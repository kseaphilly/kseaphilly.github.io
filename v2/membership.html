§ 02 — Member Activities · 회원 주요 참여 활동

Key programs and opportunities available to members.

필라델피아 지부 정회원 및 학생 회원들이 참여하는 주요 학술·네트워킹 활동입니다.

  • Annual UKC Conference
    UKC 전미 한인과학기술학술대회 참가 및 발표 지원

    Presentation support, travel grants, and networking at the premier US-Korea conference.

  • National Math & Science Competition (NMSC)
    NMSC 청소년 경시대회 (Grades 4–11) 시험 운영 및 봉사

    Volunteer as proctors, graders, and event organizers for regional STEM competitions.

  • Young Generation (YG) Network
    차세대 청년 과학기술인 및 유학생 네트워크

    Dedicated professional development, social events, and peer networking for early-career scientists.

  • KSEA Letters & Academic Publications
    KSEA Letters 학술지 논문 게재 및 열람

    Publish research articles and access national interdisciplinary scientific publications.

  • Scholarships & Academic Honors
    우수 유학생/연구자 장학금 및 학술상 수혜

    Apply for national and regional graduate student scholarships and achievement awards.

  • Industry & Career Mentorship
    산학 협력 프로젝트, 구인·구직 추천 및 커리어 자문

    Receive career mentorship, resume reviews, and direct job referrals in biotech & engineering.

  • Specialized Symposiums & Workshops
    바이오, AI 등 최신 융합 기술 워크숍

    Attend technical symposia spanning Penn Medicine, CHOP, Jefferson, and Drexel ecosystems.

  • Chapter Regular Seminars
    격주 PKSA/KITEE 합동 학술 세미나 및 네트워킹

    Participate in bi-weekly joint academic talks with UPenn, CHOP, and regional partner societies.

§ 03 — Membership Benefits · 정회원 핵심 혜택

Why joining KSEA matters.

KSEA 정회원에게 제공되는 차별화된 학술, 커리어 및 지부 혜택입니다.

  • Academic & Leadership Roles
    13개 기술 분과 및 14개 상임위원회 참여 자격

    Join technical divisions, vote in national elections, and serve on executive committees.

  • US-Korea Scientific Network
    한미 과학기술 네트워크 인프라 교류

    Connect directly with researchers across top US universities, national labs, and Korean institutes.

  • Community Outreach & Mentorship
    청소년 멘토링 및 지역 커뮤니티 기여

    Engage in youth STEM education and contribute expertise to local community initiatives.

  • Exclusive Career & Job Board
    산학 채용 기회 및 전문 포지션 정보 우선 공유

    Access member-only career postings, faculty openings, and industry recruitment drives.

  • HQ Facilities Access in Washington, D.C.
    워싱턴 D.C. KSEA 본부 오피스 및 회의실 이용

    Utilize KSEA National Headquarters office spaces and meeting rooms when visiting D.C.

  • National Recognition & Awards
    KSEA Awards 공로상 및 우수연구자상 지원 자격

    Eligibility for KSEA National Distinguished Service Awards and Outstanding Researcher Honors.

  • Direct Local Reinvestment
    납부 회비의 필라델피아 지부 행사 및 회원 환원

    The majority of membership dues paid are returned to the Philadelphia Chapter to fund local events, meals, and student travel grants.
