§ 01 — Governance

Chapter Bylaws.

The governing rules of the KSEA Philadelphia Chapter, adopted in accordance with the bylaws of the Korean‑American Scientists and Engineers Association.

Last revised: 2024 · Next review: 2026 Annual General Meeting

Article IName and affiliation

The name of this organization shall be the KSEA Philadelphia Chapter (hereafter, "the Chapter"), a local chapter of the Korean-American Scientists and Engineers Association ("KSEA National"), headquartered in the greater Philadelphia metropolitan area.

Article IIPurpose

  1. To promote the application of science and technology for the general welfare of society.
  2. To foster international cooperation, particularly between the United States and Korea, in scientific and engineering fields.
  3. To support the professional development of Korean-American scientists and engineers in the Delaware Valley region.
  4. To serve the Korean-American community through outreach, mentorship, and educational programs such as NMSC and the Young Generation program.

Article IIIMembership

Section 1 — Eligibility

Membership is open to any person of Korean heritage or interest working or studying in science, technology, engineering, or related fields in the Philadelphia region, subject to KSEA National membership standards.

Section 2 — Classes of membership

  • Regular members — $35 / year
  • Graduate members — $15 / year
  • Undergraduate members — $0–$15 / year
  • Lifetime members — one-time contribution of $525 or less per age-based schedule

Article IVOfficers and duties

The Chapter shall be administered by an Executive Board consisting of a President, Vice President, Executive Director, Finance Director, General Director, Membership Director, and such other officers as the Board determines necessary. Each officer serves a term of one year, renewable by vote.

Article VMeetings and elections

The Chapter shall hold at least one general membership meeting per calendar year. Elections for officer positions shall be conducted at the Annual General Meeting. A quorum consists of no fewer than one-third of voting members present or represented by proxy.

Article VIAmendments

These bylaws may be amended by a two-thirds vote of voting members at any general meeting, provided notice of the proposed amendment has been circulated at least fourteen (14) days in advance.
