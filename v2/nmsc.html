NMSC
§ 01 — Competition

National Math & Science Competition.

KSEA's longest-running K-12 program. The Philadelphia chapter administers the regional qualifier for grades 4–11 students across the Delaware Valley.

▸ About NMSC

A nationwide competition since 1983.

Each spring, thousands of students compete in mathematics and science exams across KSEA's 70+ chapters. Top scorers qualify for the national finals and receive scholarships and certificates.

Regional winners from Philadelphia have consistently placed in the top 10% nationally.

§ 02 — How it worksThree rounds
Round 01

Regional qualifier

Held at Temple University in September. Written exams in math and science, scored by grade level.

Round 02

Awards ceremony

Top scorers in each grade receive gold, silver, and bronze medals at a regional ceremony in October.

Round 03

National finals

Gold medalists represent Philadelphia at the national NMSC finals. Travel grants provided.
