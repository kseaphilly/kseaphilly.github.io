Sponsors.

We gratefully acknowledge the organizations whose support makes chapter programs — seminars, NMSC, the Young Generation program, and our annual symposium — possible.

§ 02 — Principal sponsorNational foundation
Principal sponsor

Korean-US Science Cooperation Center (KUSCO).

KUSCO is KSEA's sister organization in Washington, D.C., funded by the Korean government to foster scientific cooperation between the United States and Korea. KUSCO underwrites core operating support for KSEA chapters nationwide, including seminar programming and NMSC.
