YOUNG GEN
§ 01 — Next generation

Young Generation.

A KSEA initiative dedicated to Korean-American students and early-career professionals in science & engineering. Locally run by the Philadelphia chapter in partnership with PKSA and KoGSA.

What it is

Community & career development for the next generation.

Young Generation (YG) is KSEA's flagship program for undergraduates, graduate students, and professionals within five years of finishing their terminal degree.

  • Annual YG Technical & Leadership Conference (YGTLC)
  • Katalyst — year-round mentoring with senior KSEA members
  • Local peer cohorts & career roundtables
  • Scholarships and travel grants for UKC, NSF, national meetings
Who can join

Eligibility.

  • Current undergraduates in STEM
  • Graduate students & postdocs
  • Early-career professionals (≤ 5 yrs post-degree)
  • All heritages welcome — no Korean ancestry required

§ 02 — Local leadership

Led by students, for students.

Our YG cohort is co-led by the presidents of PKSA and the UPenn Korean Graduate Student Association. We meet monthly and co-host technical seminars, career roundtables, and social events throughout the academic year.
