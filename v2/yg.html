§ 01 — Student Chapters · 청년 세대

Young Generation

The Young Generation (YG) program supports Korean-American students in science and engineering. We collaborate with student chapters at local universities to provide networking, mentorship, and career development.

§ 02 — University of Pennsylvania Active Chapter
K-PEnSA
Korean Penn Scientists and Engineers Association
Visit K-PEnSA Website →
About K-PEnSA

K-PEnSA is the graduate student chapter of KSEA at the University of Pennsylvania. They organize academic seminars, social events, and networking sessions for graduate students and postdocs.

Members benefit from connections across Penn Medicine, the School of Engineering and Applied Science, and the broader Penn research community.
