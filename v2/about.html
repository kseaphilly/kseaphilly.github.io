§ 01 — Mission & Vision

About Us

The KSEA Philadelphia Chapter is a local chapter of the Korean-American Scientists and Engineers Association (KSEA), dedicated to serving Korean-American scientists and engineers across the Philadelphia metropolitan area.

Vision

To excel as a leading Korean-American scientific and engineering organization.

  • Promote the application of science and technology for the general welfare of society.
  • Foster international cooperation especially between the U.S. and Korea.
  • Help Korean-American Scientists and Engineers develop their full career potential.
  • Serve the Korean-American Communities.
Mission

Opportunities in science, technology, and entrepreneurship.

  • International Cooperation
  • Career Development
  • Community Service

We bring together researchers and engineers from Penn Medicine, CHOP, Jefferson, Drexel, Temple, and the broader Delaware Valley biotech ecosystem.
