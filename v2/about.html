§ 01 — Overview · History · Network

About the Association & Chapter.

Established on December 11, 1971 in Washington D.C., the Korean‑American Scientists and Engineers Association (KSEA) is a 501(c)(3) non-profit national professional organization. The Philadelphia Chapter represents and connects Korean-American scientists, engineers, and researchers across Greater Philadelphia and the Delaware Valley corridor.

Vision

Excel as a leading Korean‑American scientific and industrial organization.

To contribute to social welfare and industrial development in the U.S. and Korea by promoting science, technology, and interdisciplinary collaboration:

  • Promote the application of science and technology for the general welfare of society.
  • Foster international cooperation, especially between the United States and Korea.
  • Help Korean‑American scientists and engineers develop their full career potential.
  • Serve the Korean‑American community.
Mission

Promote science, technology, & entrepreneurship opportunities.

  • Societal Welfare: Promote science, technology, and entrepreneurship for public benefit.
  • Professional Network: Enhance networking and collaboration among professionals.
  • Bilateral Cooperation: Foster international collaboration between the U.S. and Korea.
  • Career Cultivation: Support scientists and engineers to reach their peak potential.
  • Community Service: Dedicated service to Korean-American communities.

In Philadelphia, we convene faculty, postdocs, and industry engineers from UPenn, Penn Medicine, CHOP, Jefferson, Drexel, Temple, Wistar Institute, and the surrounding Delaware Valley biotech corridor.

Editorial note
"A chapter is a contract between the national body and the city it sits in. Ours is Philadelphia — five major research universities, world-class biomedical centers, and a dedicated Korean-American workforce in science and technology."

§ 05 — Flagship national programs Conferences · Grants · Competitions
Flagship Conference

UKC (US-Korea Conference)

Annual US-Korea Conference on Science, Technology & Entrepreneurship bringing together 1,000+ researchers, academic leaders, and industry pioneers. (39th UKC 2026: Florida; UKC 2027: Las Vegas).

Career Development

SEED Workshop

Scientists & Engineers Early-Career Development Workshop providing grant writing, tenure strategy, and career mentorship for postdocs and junior faculty since 2013.

Youth & Leadership

IMPACTs & KATALYST

National leadership conferences: IMPACTs for young professionals navigating modern team dynamics; KATALYST for undergraduate STEM student leadership immersion.

K-12 Competitions

NMSC & STEM Art Contest

National Math & Science Competition (Grades 3–11), High School Physics Contest (with AKPA), and nationwide K-12 STEM Art Contest fostering young STEM talent.

Research Grants

Young Investigator Grants (YIG)

KSEA's highest honor for early-career researchers (≤ 6 yrs post-PhD), awarding $10,000 annual grants in Science and Engineering at UKC.

Scholarships

Student & Postdoc Fellowships

Annual competitive awards: Undergrad Scholarships ($1,500 x 20), Graduate KUSCO Scholarships ($2,000 x 20), and Postdoctoral Fellowships ($2,500 x 5).
