§ 01 — Mission · Vision · Membership

About the Chapter.

The KSEA Philadelphia Chapter is a local chapter of the Korean‑American Scientists and Engineers Association — serving Korean‑American scientists and engineers across the greater Philadelphia region and Delaware Valley.

Vision

To excel as a leading Korean‑American scientific and engineering organization.

  • Promote the application of science and technology for the general welfare of society.
  • Foster international cooperation, especially between the United States and Korea.
  • Help Korean‑American scientists and engineers develop their full career potential.
  • Serve the Korean‑American communities.
Mission

Opportunities in science, technology and entrepreneurship.

  • International Cooperation
  • Career Development
  • Community Service

We convene researchers and engineers from Penn Medicine, CHOP, Jefferson, Drexel, Temple, and the broader Delaware Valley biotech ecosystem.

Editorial note
"A chapter is a contract between the national body and the city it sits in. Ours is Philadelphia — five major research universities, dozens of labs, and a quietly essential Korean-American workforce."
