# Sponsors & Partners

KSEA Philadelphia is supported by national organizations and local corporate partners.

## National Level
- **KSEA HQ**: The Korean-American Scientists and Engineers Association.
- **KUSCO**: Korea-US Science Cooperation Center.

## Local & Industry Partners
- **Biotech/Pharma**: KASBP (Philadelphia branch).
- **Technology**: KITEE.
- **Academic**: UPenn, Drexel, Temple, TJU researchers.

## Sponsorship Benefits
- Logo placement on Website, Newsletters, and Event Banners.
- Access to a pool of highly qualified scientists and engineers.
- Community engagement opportunities.

## Call to Action
Individuals or companies interested in sponsoring can contact the Chapter President via `contact.html`.
