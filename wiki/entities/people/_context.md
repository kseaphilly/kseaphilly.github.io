# Context: People & Leadership

This folder identifies human entities associated with KSEA Philadelphia.

## AI Constraints
- **Photo Rule**: Every entry must have a `visual` field pointing to an image in `assets/`.
- **Identity Rule**: Use standard academic/professional titles (Dr., PhD, PE) if provided.
- **Link Rule**: Cross-reference individuals with their respective organizations (e.g., [[UPenn]], [[KoGSA]]).

## Schema Mapping
- `role`: Maps to organizational hierarchy in `v2/leadership.html`.
- `affiliation`: Maps to the secondary text line in leadership cards.
- `visual`: Must be rendered as a circular crop.
