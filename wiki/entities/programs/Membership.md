# Membership Information

## Registration Overview
Welcome to the KSEA Philadelphia Chapter. Membership is managed via the National KSEA portal.

### Fee Structure
- **Regular Members**: $35
- **Graduate Students**: $15
- **Undergraduate Students**: Free (via National Student Discount)

---

## Detailed Registration Steps (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Create Account
- **Action**: Visit [ksea.org/signUp](https://www.ksea.org/signUp).
- **Screenshot Ref**: `step1_signup.png`.

### Step 2: Email Verification
- **Action**: Enter 6-digit code sent to registered email.
- **Screenshot Ref**: `step2_verify.png`.

### Step 3: Sign In
- **Action**: Log in with credentials at [ksea.org/signIn](https://www.ksea.org/signIn).

### Step 4: Membership Selection
- **Action**: Navigate to "My Profile" > "Membership Information".
- **Selection**: "Student Discount" (Green box) or "Regular $35" (Red box).

### Step 5: Payment
- **Action**: Complete payment process for $15 or $35.

### Step 6: Chapter Affiliation (Critical)
- **Action**: Go to "KSEA Information" in "My Profile".
- **Selection**: Choose **"(Council) Philadelphia"** as the local chapter.

### Step 7: Local Notification
- **Action**: Email KSEA ID to `ksea.philly.chapter@gmail.com` with Venmo/Zelle ID.

---

## Mobile Registration
Special workflow involving mobile-specific UI menus.
- **Step 1**: Login on mobile.
- **Step 2**: Tap 3-line menu icon > "My Profile".
- **Step 3**: "Membership Info" > "KSEA Information" > "(Council) Philadelphia".
