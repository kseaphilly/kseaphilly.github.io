# Joint Seminar Series

A recurring collaborative seminar program co-hosted between Philadelphia-based academic and professional organizations:
- **KSEA Philadelphia**
- **PKSA**: [[PKSA]]
- **KITEE**: [[KITEE]]
- **KKYU**: [[KKYU]]

## Program Overview
The Joint Monthly Seminar Series provides an ongoing forum for researchers, students, and practitioners across medicine, engineering, and technology. Co-organized across multiple chapters and student associations, it promotes interdisciplinary research sharing and professional networking in the Greater Philadelphia area.

## Locations
- **UPenn Smilow Center for Translational Research**
- **Levine Hall**

  - Kickoff Seminar: [[Joint-Seminar-2026-09-10]] (Sue Hyon Kim on LLMs in Liver Transplantation)
- **Previous / Concluded**: [[Joint-Seminar-Series-2025-2026]] (September 2025 – April 2026)
  - Topics: AI Diagnostics, Pharma Market, Materials Science for Clean Energy

## Objective
To bridge knowledge silos among Korean-American academic and industrial communities in Philadelphia and maintain an enduring platform for regular cross-disciplinary exchange.
