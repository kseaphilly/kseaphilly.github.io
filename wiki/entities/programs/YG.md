# Young Generation (YG)

The YG group focuses on undergraduates, graduates, and early-career professionals in the Philadelphia area.

## Goals
- Facilitate networking between students and established professionals.
- Provide career mentorship.
- Host joint social and academic events.

## Core Partners
- **UPenn KoGSA**: Korean Graduate Student Association.
- **Drexel KSA**: Korean Student Association at Drexel.
- **Temple KSA**: Korean Student Association at Temple.

## Key Events
- Fall Welcome Mixer.
- Spring YG Symposium (often co-hosted with NRC).
- Mock Interview Sessions.
