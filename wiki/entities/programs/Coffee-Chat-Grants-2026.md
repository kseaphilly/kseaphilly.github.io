# Mentor Coffee Chat Micro-Grants (2026–2027)

A chapter-subsidized micro-grant program established by the KSEA Philadelphia Chapter to connect students, postdoctoral scholars, and junior researchers with senior mentors (faculty professors, principal investigators, and corporate industry scientists) over coffee and pastries.

---

## Operating Regulations & Guidelines (Fall 2026 Pilot)

### Article 1 (Purpose)
This initiative promotes substantive academic exchange, career mentorship, and inter-generational community building between early-career researchers and established senior scientists, fostering long-term member retention and organic chapter activation in the Greater Philadelphia region.

### Article 2 (Phased Timeline & Budget)
1. **Pilot Phase (Fall 2026)**: Operates with an initial designated pilot fund of **$500** (covering approximately 50 attendee-sessions on a first-come, first-served basis).
2. **Review & Expansion (Spring 2027)**: Following evaluation of participant feedback, participation diversity, and conversion metrics, a second allocation of **$500** may be approved for the Spring 2027 semester.

### Article 3 (Group Scale & Eligibility)
1. **Headcount Constraint**: **Minimum 5 to maximum 10 attendees** per session. (Groups exceeding 10 are ineligible to preserve intimate dialogue and prevent conversion into unstructured gatherings).
2. **Membership Threshold**: **At least 50% of attendees must be active, paid KSEA Philadelphia Chapter members** at the time of application or prior to the meetup date.
3. **Anti-Monopoly Cap**: To ensure equitable distribution across institutions (UPenn, Drexel, Temple, Jefferson, CHOP, Wistar, and local industry), **any single laboratory or group may receive a maximum of one grant per semester**.

### Article 4 (Mentor Qualifications & Scope)
1. **Chapter Senior Mentors**: Active KSEA Philadelphia members who are faculty professors, PIs, or senior corporate researchers/directors.
2. **External Mentors (Fully Supported)**:
   - Regional industry professionals (e.g., GSK, Merck, J&J, Spark Therapeutics, Incyte).
   - University academic advisors and faculty mentors (including non-Korean / international PIs).
   - Mentors from neighboring KSEA chapters.
   - *Note*: As long as the 50% chapter membership ratio across the total attendee group is satisfied, external mentors are fully eligible, with their refreshments subsidized under the $10/person allocation.

### Article 5 (Approval Pathways & Matching)
1. **Fast-Track (Expedited Pre-Approval)**: Meetups featuring an active KSEA Philadelphia member as the mentor receive **immediate priority pre-approval**.
2. **Standard Review (External Mentors)**: Meetups hosting external mentors undergo a rapid 24-hour administrative review of the mentor's affiliation and meetup agenda to confirm alignment with chapter goals.
3. **Mentor Matching Support**: Student and postdoc groups lacking access to senior mentors may request a mentor match upon application (specifying target disciplines such as Bio/Pharma, AI/Tech, or Career Navigation). The chapter executive board will facilitate an introduction to an appropriate mentor from the chapter network.

### Article 6 (Reimbursement Rates & Audit)
1. **Subsidy Rate**: **$10 per verified attendee** (capped between $50 and $100 per meetup).
2. **Eligible Expenses**: Coffee, tea, and bakery/pastry items consumed during the session. (Alcohol and full meals are excluded).
3. **Audit Metric**: Total reimbursement equals `verified attendee headcount in group photo × $10`, bounded by the actual itemized receipt amount. Expenses beyond the calculated grant cap are the responsibility of the attendees.

### Article 7 (Application & Disbursement Workflow)
1. **Pre-Application**: Group organizer emails `ksea.philly.chapter@gmail.com` at least 3 business days before the event, providing:
   - Meetup title and focus topic.
   - Target date, time, and venue.
   - Attendee roster (names, affiliations, email addresses, KSEA membership IDs).
   - Mentor name, title, affiliation (or requested mentor matching field).
   - Projected headcount.
2. **Approval Notice**: Chapter confirms pre-approval via return email.
3. **Documentation Submission**: Within 7 calendar days after the meetup, the organizer submits:
   - **One clear group photo showing all attendees together**.
   - **Itemized sales receipt** showing purchased items and timestamp.
   - **Payment destination**: **Zelle account information** (email or phone number enrolled in Zelle) for direct transfer from the official chapter bank account. (Check issuance available only if Zelle is unavailable).
4. **Disbursement**: Chapter treasurer executes direct reimbursement via Zelle from the official chapter bank account within 3 to 5 business days of document verification.

## Strategic Significance
This program addresses the historical barrier of budget scarcity by establishing a bounded, high-ROI $500 pilot that incentivizes membership renewal, seeds localized micro-communities, and creates a natural recruitment pipeline for senior professionals in Greater Philadelphia.
