# About Page Content

## Mission & Vision
- **Vision**: To excel as a leading Korean-American scientific, engineering, and industrial organization, promoting science for welfare, fostering US-Korea cooperation, and helping professionals develop their potential.
- **Mission**: Provide opportunities for international cooperation, career development, and community service.

## Core Message
The KSEA Philadelphia Chapter serves Korean-American scientists and engineers in the Philadelphia metropolitan area as a branch of the National KSEA.
