# About Page Content

## History & Overview
- **Establishment**: Founded on December 11, 1971 in Washington D.C.
- **Status**: 501(c)(3) non-profit professional organization; 55th Administration (Sept 2026).
- **National Footprint**: 8,000+ active paid members (30,000+ registered), 70+ local chapters, 31 YG student groups, 16 Technical Groups, 37 Affiliated Professional Societies (APS), 150+ annual events.

## Mission & Vision
- **Vision**: To excel as a leading Korean-American scientific, engineering, and industrial organization, promoting science for welfare, fostering US-Korea cooperation, and helping professionals develop their potential.
- **5-Point Mission**:
  1. Science & Society: Promote science, technology, and entrepreneurship for general welfare.
  2. Professional Network: Enhance networking and collaboration among professionals.
  3. Bilateral Cooperation: Foster international US-Korea cooperation.
  4. Career Cultivation: Cultivate scientists and engineers to reach full potential.
  5. Community Service: Serve Korean-American communities.

## Member Demographics (September 2026)
- **Technical Expertise**: Bio (20%), Medical (11%), CS/IT (10%), Civil Eng (10%), Mech Eng (10%), Electrical Eng (9%), Chemistry & Math (12%), Chemical Eng (5%), Others (12%).
- **Career Breakdown**: Academia (63%), Industry (19%), Research Institutes (12%), Others (6%).
- **Degree Distribution**: Master's MS (31%), Bachelor's BS (24%), Doctorate PhD (23%), Students/Others (22%).
- **Geographic Distribution**: East (37%), West (28%), South (14%), Midwest (12%).

## Flagship National Programs
- **UKC**: US-Korea Conference on Science, Technology & Entrepreneurship (39th UKC 2026 in Florida, UKC 2027 in Las Vegas).
- **SEED Workshop**: Scientists & Engineers Early-Career Development Workshop.
- **IMPACTs & KATALYST**: IMPACTs for young professionals; KATALYST for undergraduate mentorship immersion.
- **NMSC & STEM Art**: National Math & Science Competition (Grades 3–11), High School Physics Contest, and K-12 STEM Art Contest.
- **Grants & Scholarships**: Young Investigator Grants (YIG $10,000 x 2), Undergrad Scholarships ($1,500 x 20), Graduate Scholarships ($2,000 x 20), Postdoc Fellowships ($2,500 x 5).
