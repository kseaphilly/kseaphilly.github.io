# Joint Seminar Series (2026–2027)

Semester-based recurring academic collaboration platform co-hosted by KSEA Philadelphia, [[PKSA]], [[KITEE]], and [[KKYU]].

## Metadata
- **Academic Year**: 2026–2027 (FY 2026–2027)
- **Schedule**: Fall 2026 & Spring 2027 Semesters
- **Locations**: UPenn Smilow Center for Translational Research & Levine Hall
- **Status**: Active / Ongoing

## Structure
The 2026–2027 series transitions to a semester-based curriculum, organized into Fall Semester and Spring Semester blocks. Each individual seminar features a distinguished or rising researcher from biomedical sciences, artificial intelligence, and engineering.
