# Context: Chapter Events

Tracking KSEA Philadelphia activities for reporting and site rendering.

## AI Constraints
- **Chronology**: All events must include a `date` (YYYY-MM-DD format).
- **Categories**: Use standard types: `Seminar`, `Competition`, `Symposium`, `Workshop`.
- **Archive Policy**: Events that have passed must have a brief recap summary for the historical log.

## Site Mapping
- Event nodes feed into `v2/events.html`.
- High-priority (Upcoming) events feed into the `ticker` and hero section of `v2/index.html`.
