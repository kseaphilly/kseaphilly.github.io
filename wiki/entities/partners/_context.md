# Context: Ecosystem Partners

This folder tracks external organizations collaborating with the chapter.

## AI Constraints
- **Link Policy**: Every partner must have an official external URL and an optional [[wikilink]] to their primary KSEA liaison in the `people/` folder.
- **Mutual Grounding**: Check for shared events (e.g., Joint Seminars) between partners and chapter events.

## Site Mapping
- Logotypes for these partners are located in `v2/assets/partners/`.
- Use `mix-blend-mode: multiply` when rendering on warm backgrounds.
