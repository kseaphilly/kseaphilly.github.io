# Bio × AI Design System (v2)

The design system used for the 2026 KSEA Philadelphia website migration.

## Color Palette
- **Navy**: `#2E3A94` (`--w-navy`)
- **Ink**: `#121212` (`--w-ink`)
- **Paper**: `#FAF6EC` (`--w-paper`)
- **Cream**: `#F4EBD9` (`--w-cream`)

## Typography
- **Primary**: `Space Grotesk` (Modern, geometric sans-serif)
- **Accents**: `JetBrains Mono` (Technical, used for badges and stats)

## Rules
- **Grid**: All main sections should follow a 1px solid border grid system.
- **Images**: Leadership photos must be `120px` diameter circles with `object-fit: cover`.
- **Buttons**: Square edges, no border-radius unless specified for specific mobile call-to-actions.
