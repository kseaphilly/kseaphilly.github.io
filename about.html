About Us

Established on December 11, 1971 in Washington D.C., the Korean-American Scientists and Engineers Association (KSEA) is a 501(c)(3) non-profit national professional organization. The KSEA Philadelphia Chapter serves Korean-American scientists, engineers, and researchers across the Greater Philadelphia metropolitan area and Delaware Valley corridor (Penn Medicine, CHOP, Jefferson, Drexel, Temple, Wistar Institute, and regional biotech hubs).

Our Vision

To excel as a leading Korean-American scientific, engineering, and industrial organization, contributing to social welfare and technological innovation in the U.S. and Korea:

  • Promote the application of science and technology for the general welfare of society.
  • Foster international cooperation especially between the U.S. and Korea.
  • Help Korean-American Scientists and Engineers develop their full career potential.
  • Serve the Korean-American Communities.

Our Mission

To provide, in the areas of science, technology and entrepreneurship, opportunities for:

  • Societal Welfare: Application of science and technology for public benefit.
  • Professional Networking: Enhancing collaboration among Korean-American professionals.
  • Bilateral Cooperation: Fostering international cooperation between the U.S. and Korea.
  • Career Cultivation: Assisting scientists and engineers to reach their full potential.
  • Community Service: Dedication to serving Korean-American communities.

Flagship National Programs

Annual Conference

UKC (US-Korea Conference)

Annual US-Korea Conference on Science, Technology & Entrepreneurship gathering 1,000+ researchers, academic leaders, and industry executives. (UKC 2026: Florida; UKC 2027: Las Vegas).

Career Development

SEED Workshop

Scientists & Engineers Early-Career Development Workshop offering grant writing, tenure strategies, and career mentorship for postdocs and junior faculty.

Leadership Development

IMPACTs & KATALYST

National leadership immersion: IMPACTs for young professionals; KATALYST for undergraduate STEM mentorship and leadership.

Youth Competitions

NMSC & STEM Art Contest

National Math & Science Competition (Grades 3–11), High School Physics Contest (with AKPA), and K-12 STEM Art Contest.

Grants & Awards

Young Investigator Grants (YIG)

KSEA's highest honor for early-career researchers (≤ 6 yrs post-PhD), awarding two $10,000 grants annually in Science and Engineering.

Scholarships

Student & Postdoc Fellowships

Undergrad Scholarships ($1,500 x 20), Graduate KUSCO Scholarships ($2,000 x 20), and Postdoctoral Fellowships ($2,500 x 5).
