Welcome to the KSEA Philadelphia Chapter

Your local hub for Korean-American scientists and engineers in the Philadelphia area. We are a chapter of the Korean-American Scientists and Engineers Association (KSEA), dedicated to fostering networking, professional development, and community among our members.
